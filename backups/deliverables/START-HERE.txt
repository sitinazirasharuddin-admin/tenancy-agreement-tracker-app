TENANCY — TEAM WORKSPACE

Open app: https://tenancy-agreement-tracker-app.vercel.app/
Design preview: https://tenancy-agreement-tracker-app.vercel.app/mockups
Public sample tracker: https://tenancy-agreement-tracker-app.vercel.app/demo

1. Create your account and confirm your email.
2. Create a team workspace. The three portfolio property names can be added automatically.
3. Add units and tenants, then create tenancy agreements.
4. Record signing, stamping and payment. Completing an agreement closes its open actions.
5. In Team settings, enter a teammate's email, create an invitation, and share the copied link. They must sign in using that email.

Admins manage invitations/roles and deletion. Members share the team's day-to-day work. Each team's records are private to its members.

Open team-workspace-mockups.html in any browser for the self-contained desktop/mobile preview. It contains fictional sample tenants only. No real tenant files were available in the three portfolio folders to import.

EMAIL SETUP: Custom SMTP was not enabled at the last verified inspection. The production sender must be configured to deliver signup/reset emails to ordinary team users. Production mailbox delivery is not yet verified.
